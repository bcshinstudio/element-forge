# Wellspring trailer

Open index.html on Pages and press Play to unlock audio. The four hosted gameplay recordings are loaded automatically from trailer/media/. Gameplay audio is muted to preserve the soundtrack. Stage 6 plays at 1x, Stage 21 at 1.5x, and Stages 31 and 33 at 2x. Scene durations are synchronized to the November 25 recordings. Space pauses in recording mode; Escape returns controls.

Sequential scenes crossfade over 1.5 seconds using independent visual layers; gameplay starts after the crossfade. Direct seeking resets the layers. Gameplay scenes reserve a short lead-in and completion hold, and the story captions focus on discovery and companionship rather than character introductions. The garden character montage is temporarily replaced with environment artwork pending visual verification of Pip.

The trailer avoids fixed chapter and stage counts so the campaign can continue to grow.
