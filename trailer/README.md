# Wellspring trailer

Open index.html on Pages and press Play to unlock audio. The four hosted gameplay recordings are loaded automatically from trailer/media/. Gameplay audio is muted to preserve the soundtrack. Stage 6 plays at 1x, Stage 21 at 1.5x, and Stages 31 and 33 at 2x. Scene durations are synchronized to the November 25 recordings. Space pauses in recording mode; Escape returns controls.
