# Chapter Seven · The Shattered Abyss

After the Grand Symphony, the Conduit Weaver descends into a fracture beneath the World-Engines to stabilize the Null-Engine. The chapter adds stages 73–84 and supports the campaign’s first seven-current board. Earlier unlocks, scores, and saves carry forward.

| Stage | Verified construction and adjustments |
| --- | --- |
| 73 · The Rift-Scarred Descent | Wind uses a bridge beside a dry intersection with Water. Shadow uses the portal. A tile on the Fire approach was opened. |
| 74 · Obsidian Fracture | Earth tunnels, Ice bridges, and Plant crosses Light. Electricity stays dry. |
| 75 · Null-Current Sluice | Plant takes the bridge. Electricity crosses Water on a dry tile, and Metal uses a portal on buildable ground. One gate moves to the dry Electricity approach and several wall tiles open up. |
| 76 · The Umbral Pendulum | Earth tunnels, Shadow portals, and Light crosses Ice. The board follows the six-gate variant that keeps the southwestern Wind gate. |
| 77 · Six-Fold Abyssal Terrace | Wind uses a bridge beside a dry Water crossing and jumps the southern gap. A tile opens on Fire’s upper route. |
| 78 · Gears of the Deep | Metal tunnels through buildable mouths. Shadow takes the paired portals into the lower vault. |
| 79 · Bramble-Void Bridge | Plant takes the bridge, Wind jumps the rift, and Plant and Light cross. |
| 80 · The Sunken Monolith | Metal’s tunnel shifts west of the Water channel. Wind uses the bridge and finishes at (11,2). Ice starts at (10,6). Electricity crosses Water on a dry tile. |
| 81 · Prism-Prison Labyrinth | Earth tunnels, Shadow portals, and Light crosses Electricity. A northwestern wall tile opens for access. |
| 82 · The Corrupted Manifold | Wind uses a bridge beside its dry Water intersection. Shadow uses the portal. Three approach walls open. |
| 83 · The Abyssal Gatekeeper | Metal tunnels, Shadow uses the portal and bridge, and Electricity crosses Water on a dry tile. |
| 84 · The Null-Engine Core | Seven currents and seven active gates in the verified clear. Wind bridges before crossing Water, Earth crosses Water at the other dry intersection, and Metal uses a tunnel with a buildable entrance beside its source. Shadow uses the portal. The Metal checkpoint and two gates move, and Shadow’s southeast approach has one more covered tile. |

The verified clears use every supplied tool and visit every checkpoint. Some earlier gates can be bypassed. The coordinates are in `shattered-abyss.js` and the replayable clears are in `tests/fixtures/shattered-abyss-solutions.json`.
