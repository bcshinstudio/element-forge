# Chapter Five · The Celestial Confluence

A bridge of crystallized starlight rises from the restored Astral Loom to the builders’ highest sanctum. The Conduit Weaver aligns the outer rings, untangles the inner locks, and reunites six currents in the Star-Heart Engine.

Stages 49–60 retain the supplied endpoints, elemental pair counts, gate and checkpoint counts, and construction budgets. All boards are 12×12. The verified clears use every supplied tool and visit every checkpoint. Some gates can be bypassed on earlier boards. Difficulty labels are playtest targets.
