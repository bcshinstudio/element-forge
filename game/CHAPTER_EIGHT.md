# Chapter Eight · The World-Heart Sanctuary

The Conduit Weaver rises from the sealed Shattered Abyss into the Sanctuary, where the World-Heart channels life, warmth, tides, and storms. Stages 85–96 culminate in the campaign’s first eight-current map. Earlier saves, stage IDs, and scores carry forward.

| Stage | Verified tool use and layout adjustments |
| --- | --- |

| 86 · Solar-Umbral Choir | Plant bridges and Shadow uses the portal. Light crosses Electricity. Five approach tiles open up. |
| 87 · The Geode Aqueduct | Earth tunnels through buildable mouths, Shadow portals, and Fire crosses Water on a dry tile. |

| 92 · Aether-Glass Manifold | Light uses the bridge, Shadow portals, and Electricity crosses Water on dry ground. |
| 93 · Bramble-Forge Nexus | Earth’s tunnel shifts one row south to clear its source. Shadow portals, and Metal crosses Water on dry ground. |
| 94 · The Astral Pendulum | Wind uses the bridge, Electricity crosses Water on dry ground, Metal tunnels, and Shadow portals. Two eastern gates shift to open legal approaches. |
| 95 · Gordian Sanctuary | Plant bridges, Shadow portals, and Earth and Light cross Water at separate dry intersections. |
| 96 · The World-Heart | Eight currents use every tool and pass all six gates in the verified clear. Wind bridges before the first dry Water intersection, Earth uses the second intersection, Metal tunnels, and Shadow portals. |

All 12 boards keep the draft endpoints, checkpoint counts, gate counts, and construction inventories. A few gates and checkpoints shift, some gates can be bypassed, and human difficulty remains unverified. Exact maps are in `world-heart-sanctuary.js` and the verified routes are in `tests/fixtures/world-heart-sanctuary-solutions.json`.
