# Chapter Six · The Weaver’s Playground

After restoring the Celestial Confluence, the Conduit Weaver discovers the ancient Weavers’ experimental grounds. The chapter adds stages 61–72 and carries forward existing unlocks, scores, and campaign saves.

| Stage | Puzzle and changes from the blueprint |
| --- | --- |
| 61 · The Solo Odyssey | A solo Wind course across floating islands. The final gate moves off the destination tile. |
| 62 · Yin & Yang’s Dance | Light and Shadow thread opposite halves of a mosaic. Shadow uses the portal pair. |
| 63 · The Portal Pinball | Fire uses two portal pairs and Electricity uses the third. The western plunger lane gains one opening. |
| 64 · Ice-Skating Rink | Ice circles the rink while Wind leaps across the middle. Wind and Water cross on a dry intersection. |
| 65 · Bridge Auction | The draft inventory of four bridges is available. The verified route uses one for Plant. Earth and Metal take dry lanes and intersect. |
| 66 · Pellet Forge | An original arcade maze with all nine checkpoint pellets, a side portal pair, and an intersection. Electricity starts at (9,1) instead of (9,6). |
| 67 · The Great Wall of Scree | Earth tunnels through the northern ridge. Tunnel mouths are buildable ground. Wind, Plant, and Water enter via separate openings. |
| 68 · Intersection Highway | All four intersections are used. The two southern intersections sit on dry tiles. |
| 69 · Floor Is Lava! | The island chains gain a few connecting tiles for the non-Fire currents. Shadow portals and Wind crosses Metal. |
| 70 · The One-Way Rollercoaster | All twelve detailed-map gates are present. Light takes the bridge and crosses Ice. Electricity stays dry and finishes at (5,3) instead of (5,8). |
| 71 · Pixel Art: The Phoenix | Fire and Light cross at the chest. Wind jumps the wing gap, and Shadow portals between the tail feathers. |
| 72 · The Grand Symphony | Light uses the bridge and portal. Plant uses the tunnel. Earth and Water cross on a dry intersection. Wind finishes at (9,7) instead of (8,7). |

The detailed coordinate lists determined the tool counts where the overview differed. Some gates can be bypassed. The chapter uses the existing game rules and audio. The maps are in `weavers-playground.js` and the verified clears are in `tests/fixtures/weavers-playground-solutions.json`.
